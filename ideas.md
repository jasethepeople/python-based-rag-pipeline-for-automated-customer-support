# Design exploration — Metabase Support Hub

## Three possible directions

### 1. Signal Library
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial support system that treats each answer as an annotated field note. Warm paper tones, ink-blue data marks, and evidence citations create calm authority.

**Probability:** 0.037

### 2. Diagnostic Console
**Very Brief Intro:** A low-light operational workspace with compact diagnostic panels and sharp status signals. It would feel engineered and immediate without becoming a generic cyber interface.

**Probability:** 0.082

### 3. Paper Trail
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile research-desk composition with clipped source cards, archival metadata, and generous reading space. It emphasizes clarity, provenance, and a sense of careful human assistance.

**Probability:** 0.019

## Chosen approach: Signal Library

### Design Movement
Editorial information design blended with a contemporary scientific field guide. The page should feel like a well-composed research note rather than a chatbot landing page.

### Core Principles
1. Put the answer—not a promotional headline—at the visual center.
2. Make evidence tangible through numbered source markers, provenance labels, and linked reference cards.
3. Use asymmetry and a reading rail to guide a question-to-answer-to-sources journey.
4. Reserve the signature accent for moments of confidence, verification, and action.

### Color Philosophy
The base is soft mineral white and warm parchment to reduce fatigue during long-form support reading. Deep midnight blue anchors text and navigation with technical credibility, while mineral green acts as an ownable verification signal. A muted clay accent provides human warmth for contextual notes without competing with evidence.

### Layout Paradigm
The desktop page is a vertical support desk: a slim evidence rail sits beside a broad answer canvas, with search entering from an offset header rather than a centered hero. Sections read like stacked annotated sheets, not uniform cards.

### Signature Elements
1. A large circular green confidence seal with a fine orbital ring.
2. Numbered source lozenges that visibly connect answer statements to reference cards.
3. A faint dot-matrix and ruled-paper texture behind the answer canvas.

### Interaction Philosophy
Search feels deliberate and quiet. Source markers scroll the reader to their cited card; source cards lift slightly on hover and open the original public documentation in a new tab. The example question chips replace the query and reframe the answer preview.

### Animation
On load, the query field, confidence seal, and answer paragraphs enter in a restrained 40–70ms stagger using opacity and a small vertical translation. Hover states use a 160ms ease-out lift or color shift. All non-essential movement is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for the answer headline and short editorial questions, paired with **Manrope** for UI, metadata, and body copy. Source labels are compact uppercase Manrope with generous tracking. Numbers in markers use tabular numerals.

### Brand Essence
**A source-first support workspace for teams who need answers they can verify.**

Personality: measured, intelligent, reassuring.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and evidence-led; CTAs feel like invitations to inspect rather than sales prompts; microcopy names system state plainly.

Example lines: “Answers with a paper trail.”

Example lines: “Open the evidence behind this reply.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark combines a compact “signal ring” graphic—a split circular orbit with a centered dot—with a custom-spaced SUPPORT / LAB lock-up. The emblem stands alone in the navigation and favicon, never reduced to a generic chat bubble.

### Signature Brand Color
**Evidence Green — `#127E66`**

## Style Decisions

- The first viewport prioritizes the ask-and-answer workflow; editorial framing is intentionally quieter than the source-linked response canvas.
- **Signal Library** is the sole public-facing identity, consistently paired with the recurring signal-ring emblem.
- Source references are treated as slightly irregular archival sheets with visible citation metadata, rather than uniform feature cards.
